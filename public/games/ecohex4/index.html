Greetings eco-engineer!!!
You have been tasked with building an ecosystem.
Each turn you will be offered a choice of two organisms -- either mushroom,
plant, herbivore or carnivore --which score points when they are placed
according to the presence or absence of organisms in the adjacent squares.
Click on an entity to choose it, then click on an empty space
on the board to place the entity there.
Game is over at the end of the fifteenth turn.
Good luck!
