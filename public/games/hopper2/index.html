Help Hopper collect friends. Each turn Hopper may jump over
a square to land on the square beyond. If Hopper lands on a
mushroom, then collect that mushroom's pigment. If Hopper
lands on a friend and has the necessary pigments, then collect
that friend and score points. Hopper may not store more than two
each of red, yellow and blue pigments, nor more than one white
and one black pigment. Hopper may jump over but not land on
a web. Game is over when Hopper has no more moves.
Score at least forty points to win!
