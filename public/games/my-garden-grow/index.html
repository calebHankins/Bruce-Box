INSTRUCTIONS
You have five years to make your garden a place of pride.
At the beginning of each year you can see the lay-out of your garden
and your tools. Your tool shed is a mess, so your tools become available
in random order.
Each object in the garden shows how much effort it takes to remove it.
Each tool shows its utility. You may either place a tool in an empty space
or on top of an object adjacent to tools whose combined utility is
at least equal to the effort of that object.
Objects removed are displayed below the game board.
Spaces in which you may place a tool are outlined in red.
If you do not wish to place a tool, you may instead sell it.
Each year ends when you have either placed or sold each of your tools.
YEAR END SCORING
For each small stone you remove, you receive 1 coin.
For each complete bouquet of red, yelow and blue flowers you remove,
you receive 5 coins.
For each large rock you remove, you receive 2 pride.
For each stump you remove, you receive 3 pride.
Weeds score nothing, but each weed in your garden at the end of the year
subtracts 1 pride. Weeds are harder to remove when in clumps
and weeds may invade adjacent flower beds.
BUYING MORE TOOLS
At the end of each year, you may spend your coins to buy more tools.
Your shed may only house fifteen tools, so it may be useful to sell
some of the lower value ones during the year to leave space to buy
more useful ones.
END OF GAME
The game is over at the end of the fifth year
You win if you score at least 20 pride.
