A small collection of original browser games made with care by Bruce Baskir.

The Games

EcoHex 4

v4
Ecosystem puzzle

Greetings, eco-engineer! You have been tasked with building an ecosystem. Each turn you will be offered a choice of two organisms -- mushroom, plant, herbivore, or carnivore -- which score points based on what's in the adjacent squares. Game is over at the end of the fifteenth turn. Good luck!

Play EcoHex 4 →

Hopper 2

v2
Hop & collect

Help Hopper collect friends. Each turn Hopper may jump over a square to land on the square beyond. Land on a mushroom to collect its pigment. Land on a friend with the necessary pigments and bring them home. Game is over when Hopper has no more moves. Score at least forty points to win!

Play Hopper 2 →

My Garden Grow

v1
Garden sim

You have five years to make your garden a place of pride. Your tool shed is a mess, so your tools become available in random order. Place them to clear stones, rocks, stumps, and weeds. At the end of each year, spend your coins to buy more tools. You win if you score at least 20 pride.

Play My Garden Grow →

Smother 3

v3
Maze evasion

Use the arrow keys to move to the star at the lower right. Beware -- when the walls awake, they will move and try to trap you!

Play Smother 3 →
